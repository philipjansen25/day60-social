# Day60 Social Playbook

Social's working memory between weeks. Updated each week after HQ's Thursday brief.
Last updated: Sun, Nov 1, 2026 (first version, before any results).

---

## 1. What we're selling, in one breath

Day60 is a free 60-day job-search sprint. Paste a job link, and Day60 finds the four people closest to the hire (hiring manager, their boss, an internal champion, the recruiter), their verified work emails, and drafts a short note to each in your voice. Tick checks every draft. You send every message. Day60 never logs in to LinkedIn and never sends anything.

- **Goal of every post:** free sprint signups, via **getday60.com/try** (one job free, no account).
- **Offer:** free sprint, no card. The first 100 free sprints are capped. Never "free forever", "always free" or "lifetime". No prices.
- **Audience:** anyone looking for work, from a first job to a senior role, in any field. They read on phones and arrive mostly from Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.

## 2. Audience insights (start here, update with data)

| Who | What they feel | What lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laid off, mid-career | Shame, silence, "I've never been out this long" | Calm, practical next step; dignity; no hype |
| First job / new grad | "Everyone wants 3 years' experience" | Permission to message people; scripts they can copy |
| Senior / leadership | Applying feels beneath them; network is thin in a new field | Peer tone, the boss's-boss angle, credible stats |
| Career changers | No one in the new field knows them | The internal champion; a sharp question beats a résumé |
| Friends and family (FB) | Want to help someone they love who's looking | Share/tag asks: "send this to someone looking" |

Working hypotheses to test (not facts yet):
- The pain is **silence after applying**, not lack of jobs. Hooks that name the silence should beat hooks that name the product.
- "Messaging the hiring manager feels pushy" is the biggest objection. Posts that answer it should earn comments.
- Saves come from copyable lines (scripts, one-liners). Shares come from relatable pain.

## 3. Content pillars

Every post belongs to one pillar. Aim for a mix each week; no pillar more than 40%.

1. **The Pile** (awareness). Why the apply button fails, in plain numbers and relatable moments. The 3.1% stat lives here. Goal: reach and shares.
2. **The Four People** (teach). One person at a time: who they are, what they care about, one line that gets them to read. Goal: saves.
3. **Tick's Red Pen** (teach + product). Real-sounding messages (labeled illustrative) cut down live: before/after, banned phrases, the "could you paste this into another company's inbox?" test. Goal: saves and comments. This is our signature pillar.
4. **Receipts** (proof). Philip's own search (IG, FB, TikTok only, never LinkedIn), spots-left counts, user wins shared with written permission. Goal: signups.
5. **Inside the Sprint** (product + personality). Screen recordings of /try, Tick being Tick, Philip building in public. Goal: signups and follows.

## 4. Signature formats per platform

Every idea gets reshaped per platform. Never paste one post across channels.

### LinkedIn (Philip's personal profile)
- **Field note:** text-first post, 120–220 words, one idea, short lines. Hook line under 60 characters, so it shows before "...more". Opinion from nine years of building SDR teams, applied to job seekers. One stat card image or none.
- **PDF carousel (document post):** 5–7 slides, 1080×1350, same brand system as Instagram. Use for The Four People and Tick's Red Pen.
- Rules: credible and professional, peer to peer. **Never Philip's 39/27 result while he is interviewing.** End on a question that invites a real answer. `getday60.com/try` named in the post; the UTM link goes in the first comment.

### Instagram (@getday60)
- **Save-this carousel:** 6–8 slides. Slide 1 is a lime or cream cover with a bold hook and a "SAVE THIS" pill. Last slide is the CTA: "Paste a job link. Get your 4 people. Link in bio."
- **Text-on-screen Reel** (7–20 s): a hook in frame 1, a message getting cut by Tick's red pen, end card with the CTA. Rendered from HTML with Playwright.
- Rules: the visual does the work. Caption hook in the first line. "Link in bio" (no clickable links). Five hashtags.

### Facebook (Day60 Page)
- **"Which would you answer?"** A/B image plus a question; comments come easily.
- **Send-this-to-someone post:** warm, plain tip written for the friend or parent of someone looking, ending with "Share this with someone who's looking."
- Rules: links are clickable, so use the full UTM link in the post. Warmer and more personal than LinkedIn.

### TikTok (@getday60; Philip posts by hand until Buffer connects it)
- **"Tick checks your message":** green-screen-style text video or screen recording. A cringe opener ("I hope this finds you well") gets flagged line by line. Hook on screen in the first second.
- **"Paste a job link, watch":** 15–25 s screen recording of /try finding the four people (names and emails blurred). Payoff by second 3.
- **Pattern-interrupt one-liners:** "POV: you've applied to 200 jobs and your inbox is a ghost town." Trend sounds when they fit; original audio otherwise.
- Rules: entertainment first, lesson second, CTA last ("link in bio"). 3–5 hashtags.

### Weekly founder video (Philip films himself, 30 s)
Second 0–1: hook spoken and on screen. 1–22: one point, one example. 22–30: CTA with on-screen text. Phone upright, window light, one take. Works for IG Reels, TikTok and FB. LinkedIn only if it contains no personal results.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| TikTok | 5 | 7:00–9:00 PM | Hand-posted until connected |
| Founder video | 1 | Script delivered with the week | Philip films |

- Week runs Sunday through Saturday. Skip national news days (e.g., Election Day, Nov 3) and be careful on holidays (Veterans Day Nov 11, Thanksgiving Nov 26).
- **Buffer limit:** the current plan allows **10 scheduled posts at a time** and **3 channels**. A full week (3 LI + 4 IG + 3 FB) is exactly 10, so next week can't be fully queued until this week's posts have gone out. TikTok needs a 4th channel slot (upgrade pending).

## 6. Hashtag sets

Hashtags go on every post on every platform. Rotate within a set; put them at the end of the caption.

**LinkedIn (3 max):** `#jobsearch` + two from `#careers #hiring #networking #careeradvice #recruiting`. Use `#salesdevelopment` only on SDR-playbook posts.

**Instagram (5; Instagram caps posts at 5):** always `#day60` + 4 from one set:
- Tactics: `#jobsearch #jobsearchtips #careertips #networking`
- Messages: `#jobsearch #careeradvice #networkingtips #coverletter`
- Proof/story: `#jobhunting #jobsearch #newjob #careeradvice`
- Early career: `#firstjob #entryleveljobs #jobsearchtips #careertips`

**Facebook (2–3):** `#jobsearch #day60` + one of `#jobhunting #careeradvice #laidoff`.

**TikTok (3–5):** `#jobtok #jobsearch #day60` + up to two of `#careertok #jobsearchtips #corporatetok #firstjob #laidoff`.

## 7. UTM and link conventions

- Base: `https://getday60.com/try?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<YYYY>-w<WW>&utm_content=<post-slug>`
  - `utm_source`: `linkedin`, `instagram`, `facebook`, `tiktok`
  - `utm_campaign`: the ISO week of the Monday inside our Sunday–Saturday week. Nov 8–14, 2026 = `2026-w46`; Nov 15–21 = `2026-w47`.
  - `utm_content`: short post slug, e.g. `boss-carousel`, so we can trace single posts.
- Pre-filled job: `https://getday60.com/try?jobUrl=<URL-encoded job link>&utm_source=...` for posts that demo a specific public job.
- LinkedIn: say `getday60.com/try` in the post; the full UTM link goes in the first comment.
- Instagram and TikTok: "link in bio". The bio link carries `utm_source=instagram` / `tiktok`, `utm_medium=social` and the current week's `utm_campaign` (to be confirmed by Philip, who updates the bio each Sunday).
- Facebook: full UTM link in the post body.
- Week 1 (Nov 2–6) used an older pattern (`utm_campaign=wk1_<slug>`, LinkedIn with no UTM). From week 46 on, everything follows the rule above.

## 8. Repo and file conventions

- Images: `YYYY-MM/wkWW/` where WW is the ISO week number used in `utm_campaign` (week of Nov 8 = `2026-11/wk46/`). Week 1's folder `2026-11/wk1/` stays as-is because Buffer links to it.
- Filenames: `pNN_<slug>_<n>.png`, 1080×1350 for feed images and carousels, 1080×1920 for Reels/TikTok (MP4).
- Each week's copy lives in `YYYY-MM/wkWW/posts.md`: platform, day, time (Central), copy, image paths, hashtags, link.
- Never rename or delete a file that a scheduled post uses.

## 9. Visual rules (from the brand kit)

- Colors: cream `#FFF8EC` background, ink `#16133A` text and outlines, lime `#D9FF3F` highlight, coral `#FF5C4D`, blue `#3B5BFF`, pink `#FF9BDB` accents.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for headlines (tight tracking), DM Sans 500/700 for body.
- Shapes: thick ink outlines, rounded corners, hard offset shadows (no blur), four-point sparkles, pill labels in caps.
- Tick appears on most covers: running (momentum), celebrating (wins), waiting (the pile), thinking (tips).
- Footer: Day60 logo bottom left, getday60.com bottom right, slide count on carousels.
- Text big enough to read on a phone at arm's length: headlines 90px+, body 34px+ on a 1080 canvas.

## 10. Voice notes

- Fun, vibrant, confident, plain English. Think a friend who used to run sales teams and is weirdly good at this.
- Short sentences. Specific nouns and numbers. One idea per post.
- Talk to one person ("you"), not "job seekers".
- Philip's own voice (from his LinkedIn history): punchy single lines, numbers, a little self-deprecating humor, the occasional "s/o", and very few emojis. Borrow the rhythm, not the emoji count.
- Product words: "the sprint", "the Day60 Sprint", "job" or "target job". Day60 "finds" the four people and their verified work emails, "drafts" notes, and Tick "checks" them. The user "sends".
- Honesty lines: we never promise replies, interviews or offers. Philip's result is "my results, yours will vary". Invented examples are labeled "Illustrative example". Stats carry their source.

## 11. Banned phrases

Cut on sight in final copy (fine only inside a quoted "bad message" example being torn down).

**Off-brand or untrue for Day60:** campaign (for a job), AI-powered, revolutionary, supercharge, unlock, game-changer, free forever, always free, lifetime, guaranteed, land your dream job, hack/hacks, automate your job search, we send for you, auto-apply, mass outreach, blast.

**AI giveaways:** delve, tapestry, in today's fast-paced world, in today's job market, navigate the landscape, it's not just X, it's Y (and "isn't about X, it's about Y"), elevate, leverage (as a verb), seamless, harness, embark, journey (for a job search), realm, robust, boost your productivity, let's dive in, buckle up, here's the thing, the secret sauce, a testament to. Avoid em-dashes; use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.

**Hype and spam:** 🚀 strings, ALL-CAPS sentences, "Don't miss out", "Act now", "Limited time only" (use the real cap: "the first 100 free sprints"), "you won't believe", "this changes everything".

**Job-seeker clichés we mock, never use straight:** I hope this finds you well, I'm passionate about, I'd love to pick your brain, just following up, to whom it may concern, please find my résumé attached, I'm a great fit.
