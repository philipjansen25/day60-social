A short guide to holiday out-of-office replies.

"Back on January 4": back on January 6.
"Limited access to email": full access, zero intention.
"For urgent matters, contact my colleague": the colleague is also out.
"I'll respond when I return": to the newest email first.

Here's the useful part. An out-of-office reply is information, not a rejection. It tells you when to follow up, and sometimes it names who covers the team.

So when you get one, don't resend. Note the return date and put a follow-up on your calendar for the day after. If the reply names a colleague, that's a new name worth knowing.
