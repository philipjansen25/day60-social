You've done the job for years. You apply online, and you hear nothing.

Experience isn't the problem. The pile is.

Day60 is the part after you find the job: reaching the people who decide. Paste a job link. Day60 finds the four people closest to the hire: the hiring manager, their boss, an internal champion and the recruiter, and a verified work email for each when it can find one. It drafts a short note to each, in your voice. Tick checks every draft against plain writing rules. You send it.

It runs as a 60-day sprint. Start with three target jobs a week, and add up to 3 a day when you find more good ones. You work a short list properly instead of feeding a pile.

There's also a free resume builder. Add a job and your resume, and it tailors the resume to that one posting, using only what your resume and your notes show. Add your email to get a Word file. Your resume isn't stored.

1. They ask: How does it find people?
------------------------------------------------------------
It works from the posting itself (company, team and title) to find the four people closest to that hire, and a verified work email for each when it can find one. You see every person and every note, and nothing goes out unless you send it.
------------------------------------------------------------

2. They ask: What does it cost?
------------------------------------------------------------
$79 for a 60-day sprint, and $39 if you run another one after that. You can try one job free first at getday60.com/try, no account needed. You see every person and every note before anything goes out, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
------------------------------------------------------------

3. They ask: LinkedIn and privacy?
------------------------------------------------------------
Day60 never logs in to LinkedIn and never sends anything. The Chrome extension never reads LinkedIn pages. On a LinkedIn job or profile it uses only the tab's address and title, and only when you press its button. The extension has no analytics.
------------------------------------------------------------

4. They ask: Who is it for?
------------------------------------------------------------
It's built for people a few years into their careers who apply online and hear nothing back, in tech, sales, marketing, operations or corporate roles. It works for anyone looking for work. This launch is for people with real experience whose applications sit in a pile.
------------------------------------------------------------

5. They ask: What does Tick check?
------------------------------------------------------------
His rules include: Doesn't start with "I". Short subject line. Asks one clear question. The question comes last. No overused phrases. No private details. No links or attachments. He checks the rules, not the facts, so make sure every detail is true. The checker is free on its own: getday60.com/message-checker.
